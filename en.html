About the App

"Post-Credit" is a fast, ad-free, and completely free mobile guide designed for moviegoers to decide whether to wait in the theater after the movie ends. Powered by an advanced movie database (TMDB), it reports in seconds whether there is a mid-credits or post-credits scene.

Key Features

  • Fast and Clear Results: No complex menus, just search and know instantly.
  • Waiting Guide: Instantly shows whether you need to stay in your seat for the credits.
  • Dual Language Support: Seamlessly switch between Turkish and English.
  • Ad-Free and Secure: Focused purely on personal use with a distraction-free interface.
