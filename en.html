About the App

"Any Post-Credits?" is a fast, ad-free, and completely free mobile guide designed for moviegoers to decide whether to wait in the theater after the movie ends. Powered by an advanced movie database (TMDB), it reports in seconds whether there is a mid-credits or post-credits scene.

Key Features

  • Fast and Clear Results: No complex menus, just search and know instantly.
  • Detailed Timing: Shows whether scenes are mid-credits or at the very end.
  • Dual Language Support: Seamlessly switch between Turkish and English.
  • Ad-Free and Secure: Focused purely on personal use with a distraction-free interface.

Gizlilik Politikası / Privacy Policy

This application ("Any Post-Credits?") does not collect, record, or share any personal data from its users with third parties.

Movie searches made within the application are processed only instantly to fetch results from the movie database.
